# Timeline

This timeline includes only entries for which a useful primary or near-primary source has been identified. It will expand as references are verified.

## 1998 — Bimanual curve-editing research

Owen, Kurtenbach, Fitzmaurice, Baudel, and Buxton discuss two-handed input and explicitly describe the general idea of two input devices such as two mice, one for each hand, with two cursors.

See: [papers.md](papers.md)

## 2000 — Two mice / two cursors in collaborative puzzle research

Graphics Interface 2000 publishes *Towards Seamless Support of Natural Collaborative Interactions*. One experimental condition gives each child a mouse and a cursor, allowing synchronous independent interaction.

## 2000-2002 — Multiple-cursor patent prior art

Sony-associated patent family with priority in 2000 describes multiple cursors and multiple pointing devices, including multiple mice.

## 2006 — Symmetric bimanual interaction thesis

Celine Latulipe's University of Waterloo PhD thesis develops a symmetric interaction model for bimanual input.

## Cross-platform library era — ManyMouse

ManyMouse provides a small C abstraction for reading multiple mice separately across Windows, Linux, and macOS. Exact historical release milestones still need to be documented.

## 2025 — Shatterball

An alpha roguelike brick-breaker supports dual-mouse control, with each breaker controlled independently.

## 2026 — MouseMux Multi Mini Games

MouseMux documents a shared-canvas collection with multiple colored cursors and 24 mini-games, including Asteroids.

## 2026 — Turnip Mountain

Released July 30, 2026. The commercial physics climbing game supports two mice, one for each independently controlled hand.

## 2026 — DualOperation

A working Unreal Engine dual-input framework publicly documents one player using two physical mice as independently routed operators with separate cursors/reticles and gameplay contexts.
