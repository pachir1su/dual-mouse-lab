# Patents and Prior Art

This file is for historical/prior-art research, **not legal conclusions**.

## US20020059308A1 / US7620900B2

**Display control apparatus, method for controlling display of information, and recording medium recorded program for such method**

- Priority date: 2000-04-27
- Inventors: Isao Kawashima, Mario Tokoro
- Assignee history includes Sony Corporation
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20020059308A1/en

The disclosure describes multiple cursors independently operated by users and explicitly discusses terminals with multiple pointing devices such as multiple mice or tablets. This is strong evidence that the broad concept of multiple pointing devices controlling multiple cursors predates this project by decades.

The listed Google Patents status should not be treated as a legal opinion.

## Research method

For future patent entries, record:

- publication/application number
- priority date
- inventors
- assignee
- current listed status
- exact section relevant to multiple pointing devices/cursors
- whether it concerns one user with two hands or multiple users
- direct patent database URL

## Caution

Do not add a patent just because it contains the word “cursor.” The relevant claim or description must actually concern multiple independent pointing inputs, cursors, or bimanual pointer interaction.
